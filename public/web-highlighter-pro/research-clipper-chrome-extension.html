A research clipper for Chrome.

Web Highlighter Pro turns selected webpage text into reusable research notes. Save quotes locally, keep source URLs attached, search old highlights, and export everything when ready.

Research clipping without another account

Many web clippers become full note systems. Web Highlighter Pro stays smaller: capture useful text, organize it by source website, and export to your own workflow.

Best for:
  • Students saving study passages
  • Researchers collecting citations and source material
  • Writers collecting references
  • Founders saving customer, competitor, and market quotes
  • PKM users exporting notes to Markdown

Local-first workflow

Saved highlights live in Chrome local storage. You can use the extension without creating an account or syncing notes to a separate database.
