Local-first web highlighting.

Web Highlighter Pro is a Chrome extension for people who want a lightweight web highlighter without a required account, sync system, or backend notes database.

What local-first means here

Your saved quotes are stored in Chrome local storage. You can search them from the popup, filter them by source website, and export them as Markdown or TXT files.

Use it for:
  • Saving research snippets locally
  • Collecting webpage quotes with source URLs
  • Exporting highlights into your own note system
  • Avoiding another account for simple clipping

Designed for export

The goal is not to trap your notes. Export saved highlights and keep using Obsidian, Notion, Logseq, Bear, Google Docs, or plain Markdown files.
